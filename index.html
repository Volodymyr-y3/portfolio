Aura — Product Delivery & Release Transformation

Agile Delivery Manager · Nov 2019–Oct 2021
4 teams · 24 FTE · 5 products

Context

Aura operated a consumer cybersecurity and privacy portfolio across iOS, macOS, Android and Windows, with release and coordination processes limiting throughput.

My ownership

  • Managed delivery across four product teams / 24 FTE.
  • Coordinated Product, Engineering, QA and Design.
  • Improved planning, release governance and dependency management.
  • Strengthened recruitment and onboarding to support capacity.

Delivery problem

The goal was not simply to accelerate one launch, but to create a repeatable operating model that enabled teams to ship more frequently.

Outcome

Release cycles moved from approximately 6–8 weeks toward two weeks across five products and three platforms. Release testing reduced from ~5 days to 2–3 days, and feature lead time from ~12 weeks to four weeks.
