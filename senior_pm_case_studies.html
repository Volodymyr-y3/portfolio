2. Aura — Scaling Product Delivery & Release Management

Role: Agile Delivery Manager | Period: Nov 2019–Oct 2021

4

24

5

2 weeks

Business context

Aura operated multiple consumer cybersecurity and privacy products across iOS, macOS, Android and Windows. Delivery processes were limiting the frequency and predictability of releases.

My role

  • Managed delivery across Product, Engineering, QA and Design.
  • Improved planning, release governance, dependency management and team coordination.
  • Worked on recruitment and onboarding processes to support sustainable team capacity.
  • Introduced stronger pre-release smoke and regression processes.
  • Tracked delivery and quality metrics to identify process bottlenecks.

Challenge

Products were operating on longer release cycles, with process and coordination overhead limiting throughput. The goal was to create a repeatable delivery model rather than accelerate a single release.

Outcome

The organisation moved from approximately 6–8 week release cycles toward two-week releases across five products and three platforms. Release testing time was reduced from approximately five days to 2–3 days through improvements to the testing and release process. Feature lead time was reduced from approximately 12 weeks to four weeks.

The programme successfully coordinated eight teams and more than 100 engineers toward the SIXT Share launch, with clearer ownership of dependencies, release readiness and operational support.
